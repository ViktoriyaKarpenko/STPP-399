About the game

A treasure hunt across the seven seas

Pirate Match combines classic match-3 gameplay with an exciting pirate adventure. Every completed puzzle helps your crew discover new islands, recover forgotten artifacts, and unlock incredible rewards hidden throughout the ocean.

As the journey continues, players encounter ancient ruins, mysterious caves, and legendary treasures waiting to be claimed.
